Rider setup — for calorie estimate
How this works: the bike reports pack voltage and current, so the app knows roughly what the motor contributed. It works out the power needed to move you at your speed, subtracts the motor's share, and treats the remainder as your legs. Throttle along without pedalling and it should read near zero.

It is an estimate, not a measurement. Your bike has no pedal-torque sensor, so your effort is inferred rather than measured, and hills are approximated by the terrain setting above rather than sensed. Treat it as a decent guide to relative effort between rides, not a calorie count to diet on.
